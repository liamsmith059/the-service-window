Show art under this folder was supplied by Liam on 25 November 2026.
Each show uses a 400px square WebP plus a 200px square variant.
To replace a show's pictures, swap the two WebP files named below and
update artWidth, artHeight, artAlt, and artSource in data/podcasts.js.
Wide sources should be fitted into the square without cropping, with
empty bands filled from a colour sampled at the art's edges.
